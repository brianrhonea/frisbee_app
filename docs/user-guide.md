# UltiUP user guide

UltiUP keeps your ultimate team in one place: schedule, roster, playbook,
stats, plus USA Ultimate tournaments and rankings. This guide covers
version 0.0.4.

## Getting started

1. **Sign in** with email and password, Google or Apple. (Google and Apple
   aren't available on Windows.)
2. **Join or create a team.** On Home or the Teams tab:
   - **Join with code**: enter the 6-character invite code your team admin
     gave you. You join as a player.
   - **Create team**: you become its owner. Share the invite code from the
     team's **Members** page.
3. **Profile** opens from your avatar at the top of Home. Change your
   display name, pick a theme (System, Light or Dark), sign out or delete
   your account there.

## Roles

What you can do on a team depends on your role. Your role shows on the
team card in the Teams tab.

| You can…                                        | Owner | Coach / Captain | Player |
|-------------------------------------------------|:-----:|:---------------:|:------:|
| See team events, posts, roster names, numbers, positions | ✓ | ✓          | ✓      |
| RSVP, react and vote on team events             | ✓     | ✓               | ✓      |
| Create, edit and delete team events             | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Write posts; make posts and events public       | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Edit the roster and team details                | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| See paid and USA Ultimate status, sync roster   | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Draw and edit plays                             | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Chat in rooms you've been given access to       | ✓     | ✓               | ✓      |
| Create chat rooms and set who can read/write    | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Read every chat room, delete any message or room | ✓    |                 |        |
| Keep stats                                      | ✓     | ✓               | with stats access |
| See and share the invite code                   | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Remove players                                  | ✓     | ✓               |        |
| Remove coaches and captains, set roles          | ✓     |                 |        |
| Give a player stats access                      | ✓     |                 |        |
| Make a new invite code, transfer or delete team | ✓     |                 |        |

Anyone can leave a team from the ⋮ menu on its home page (the owner must transfer
ownership first).

## The tabs

### Home

- **This week**: the next seven days. Dots under each day show events:
  **blue for team**, **purple for personal**. Each event has a TEAM or
  PERSONAL tag (or the team's name if you're on several teams). Tap a day or
  **Full schedule** to open the Schedule tab.
- **My team**: your team at a glance. If you're on several teams, switch
  with the chips.
- **Playbook**: a preview of your team's plays. Tap one to open it.
- **Coming up**: your next team and personal events. Turn on **Show other
  events** to add upcoming USA Ultimate tournaments.
- **Rankings**: the top 3 on a podium and ranks 4–10. Switch division with
  the chips, or tap **Full rankings**.

### Notifications

Tap the 🔔 at the top of Home to see your notifications. A badge shows how
many are unread. Tap one to open it; **Mark all read** clears the badge.

You're notified about:

- **Team chat**: new messages in rooms you can read. Several messages in
  one room fold into one notification.
- **Direct messages**: new private messages.
- **Event changes**: team events added, moved, changed or cancelled (not
  RSVPs or reactions).
- **Event reminders**: a reminder about a day before each team or personal
  event, unless you've said you can't go.
- **Team activity**: new and updated plays in the playbook.
- **Meetings**: meetings you're invited to, cancellations, and a heads-up
  when one you're attending starts.

You aren't notified about things you did yourself.

**Settings** (the ⋮ menu on the notifications page, or Profile →
Notifications):

- **This device**: tap **Turn on** to get push notifications on your phone,
  tablet or Mac, even when UltiUP is closed. Windows has no push, but
  everything still shows under the 🔔.
- **Notify me about**: turn each kind on or off for all your teams. Your
  choices apply on every device you use.
- **Teams**: change any kind for one team, for example reminders only for
  your club team. **Use my defaults** undoes that.
- To mute one chat room, tap the 🔔 at the top of the room.

### Messages

Tap the 💬 icon at the top of Home to open your private messages. A badge
shows how many conversations have something new.

- **New message**: search for anyone on UltiUP by name, then tap them to
  start a conversation. You don't need to share a team.
- Only the two of you can see a conversation. Attach photos or files up to
  10 MB, the same as team chat. You can delete your own messages.
- On a computer, conversations are listed on the left with the open one
  on the right. On a phone, a conversation opens full screen; use the 💬
  button at the top left to switch between conversations.
- You show up in search once you've set a name in your Profile. Search
  shows names and photos only, never email addresses.

### Teams

Your teams. Tap one to open its home page: the team at a glance, what's
**Up next**, and the team's **Posts**. Owners, coaches and captains tap
**New post** to share news (tryouts, fees, field changes). Each post is
**Public** or for members only; admins can edit or delete posts.

Tiles on the home page open each part of the team:

- **Team Events**: the team calendar. Open an event to RSVP **Going**,
  **Maybe** or **Can't go**. Once you're going, you can react and vote in
  the event's poll. Admins can make an event **Public** to show it on the
  team's public page; RSVPs and polls stay private.
- **Chat**: the team's chat rooms. Every team has a **General** room for
  everyone. Owners, coaches and captains add rooms (say, Captains or
  Carpool) and choose, per role and per person, who can read and who can
  post. Attach photos or files up to 10 MB; tap a photo to enlarge or save
  it, tap a file to save it. You can delete your own messages; the owner can
  delete any.
  On a computer, rooms are listed on the left and the open room is on the
  right. On a phone, tap a room to slide it open; the 💬 button at the top
  left opens your room list, and tapping the open room takes you back.
- **Roster**: players with number and position. Admins also see paid and
  USA Ultimate status, and can import or sync the roster from USA Ultimate.
- **Playbook**: the team's plays. Admins draw plays on the field and add
  animation steps (drag a player to record a cut, the disc to record a
  throw); everyone can play them back.
- **Stats**: tournaments and games. Stat keepers track points live (lines,
  O/D, pull timer, subs, timeouts); everyone can view stat sheets and
  season stats.
- **Members**: who's on the team and their roles. Admins find the invite
  code here.

**Find a team**: tap 🔍 at the top of the Teams tab and type part of a
team's name or city. Tap a team to see its public page: name, division and
city, plus any public events and posts. Members, rosters and chat stay
private. Tap **Join** and enter the invite code to join.

**Search USA Ultimate rankings**: at the bottom of Find a team. Type a
team name and filter by ranking list (for example, Club Mixed). Tap a team
to see its USA Ultimate page. Only teams in the USA Ultimate rankings show
up.

### Community groups

A community group is an open pickup community: no roster or stats, just
**Events**, **Chat**, **Tournaments** and **Members**. In a group, roles
are **Owner**, **Organizer** (like a captain: runs events and
tournaments) and **Member**.

- **Start one**: Teams tab → **New** → **Community group**. Add a
  description and a location (search an address, then tap the map to
  fine-tune). Turn on **Approve new members** to review who joins.
- **Find one**: tap 🔍 in the Teams tab and choose **Groups**. Open a group
  to see its description and map, then tap **Join** (or **Ask to join**,
  if it needs approval). Organizers approve requests on the Members page.
- **Pickup** events work like any team event. For **Tournament** events
  the RSVPs are **Attending**, **Planning to attend** and **Not able to
  make it :(**.

**Running a tournament** (owners and organizers):

1. Post a Tournament event so people can RSVP. On the event, tap **Set up
   tournament** (or **New tournament** on the Tournaments page). Every
   tournament shows on the group schedule: one set up without an event
   gets a Tournament event when you save, and a linked event's title and
   times follow the tournament's. The event then has a **View** button
   for the tournament.
2. Set the **dates**: the first and last day of play. Each day gets its
   own **start time**; tap a day to change it. Add an ideal end if you
   like.
3. Pick the format (round robin, pool play + single elimination, single
   elimination, double elimination) and the number of **teams** (they
   start as Team 1, Team 2… and you can rename them), plus players on the
   field (3v3 to 7v7), game length, breaks, score cap, timeouts and fields.
4. Check the **schedule preview**. Games are split evenly over the days,
   in bracket order. It warns if games run past the ideal end. Tap
   **Publish schedule**. Teams can still be empty at this point.
5. Add players, now or any time before play: **Import RSVPs** brings in
   everyone attending or planning to attend, **Add member** adds anyone
   else, and **Assign teams** deals them all evenly into the teams at
   random, each with a random captain. Tap a player to move them or make
   them captain. Tap **Save**; the schedule stays as it is. Changing the
   number of teams means publishing the schedule again.

**Playing**: the tournament shows up for everyone on a team. Open it for
the schedule, standings, bracket and teams; your games are highlighted.
Each team's **captain** taps a game to **Report score**. The other captain
then **confirms** it, or taps **That's not right** to report their own
score for the first captain to confirm. Organizers can set or correct any
score. Once a score is final, winners (and losers, in double elimination)
move on in the bracket. With pool play, organizers tap **Pools are done:
seed the bracket** when every pool game is final.

### Schedule

Your whole week in one place: personal events plus every team you're on.

- Use the arrows to move between weeks, and **Today** to come back.
- Tap a day in the strip to jump to it.
- Filter with **All**, **Team** or **Personal**.
- **New event** adds an event on the selected day. If you're an owner,
  coach or captain, choose whether it goes on **My schedule** or a team's
  schedule.
- **Swipe an event right to delete it.** Tap **Undo** within 4 seconds to
  keep it. You can always swipe your personal events; team events only if
  you're an owner, coach or captain. Others can't be swiped.

Event types: Practice, Game, Tournament, Scrimmage, Workout, Social, Other
(in groups: Pickup, Tournament, Social, Other).
Personal events are private: only you can see them.

### Videos

Videos shared with your team, like meeting recordings or game film. Open
your team and tap **Videos**.

- **Watch:** tap a video to play it in the app. For YouTube videos,
  **Open on YouTube** plays it on YouTube instead.
- **Add one** (owners, coaches and captains):
  1. Upload the video to YouTube. Set it to **Unlisted** if it's just for
     the team, and leave **Allow embedding** on.
  2. In **Videos**, tap **Add video** and paste the link. UltiUP checks it
     with YouTube, shows the thumbnail and fills in the title.
  3. Change the title if you like, add a description, and tap
     **Add video**.
- **Or upload a video file:** tap **Add video**, choose **Upload file**,
  then **Choose video file**. MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, MPEG and 3GP
  all work. Files can be up to 500 MB, and each team has 5 GB in
  all; the bar above the videos shows how much is used. Keep the page
  open until the upload finishes.
- If UltiUP says YouTube won't play the video here, it's set to Private,
  it was deleted, or embedding is turned off. Fix that on YouTube and
  paste the link again.
- **Folders:** tap a chip above the videos (**All**, a folder, or
  **Unsorted**) to see just those. Owners, coaches and captains tap
  **New folder** to make one (say Film review, Game film or Highlights),
  and **⋮ → Move to folder** on a video to file it. With a folder open,
  **Add video** puts new videos in it, and the **⋮** next to the chips
  renames or deletes it. Deleting a folder doesn't delete its videos;
  they move to Unsorted.
- Use **⋮ → Edit** or **Remove** on a video to change or unlist it.
  Removing a YouTube video here doesn't delete it from YouTube. Removing
  an upload deletes the file and frees its space.
- Unlisted videos aren't searchable on YouTube, but anyone who has the
  link can watch, so only share film you're happy to have passed along.

### Meetings

Video meetings for your team. They show on the schedule with a 🎥 icon,
but only to the people invited.

- **Schedule one** (owners, coaches and captains): on the team's Events
  tab tap **New** → **Meeting**. Give it a title, time and agenda, then
  pick who's invited: whole roles (Admins are always in), particular
  people, or both. Turn on **Require admin approval to join** to let people
  in one at a time.
- **RSVP** on the meeting page: **Attending** or **Not attending**. Admins
  see everyone's answers.
- **Go to meeting room** opens the lobby. Pick your microphone, speaker
  and camera, check your camera preview and set the meeting volume. UltiUP
  asks for camera and microphone access the first time.
- **Starting:** an admin taps **Start meeting** (or **Start meeting early**
  before the start time). Everyone attending who isn't already there gets a
  notification, or a banner with **Join** if UltiUP is open.
- **Getting in:** without approval, tap **Join meeting** once it's started.
  With approval, tap **Ask to join** and wait; admins see the waiting list
  in the lobby and in the call, and tap **Admit** or **Deny**.
- **In the call** you'll see everyone's camera, or their photo when it's
  off; a green border shows who's talking. The buttons along the bottom:
  mic, camera, mute all sound, share screen (Windows, Mac and Android),
  chat, settings and **Leave**. Admins can also **End for everyone**.
- **Chat** lets you type instead of unmuting. Only people in the call can
  read it.
- **Watch a video together** (owners, coaches and captains): tap the
  videos button along the bottom and pick one of the team's videos (filter
  by folder). It fills the screen for everyone, with people in a strip
  beside it. Use **Play**, **Pause** and the slider; everyone's video
  follows yours. **Stop** goes back to the call. Another admin can tap
  **Take control** to drive. Everyone hears the video through their own
  device at full quality, so there's no need to share your screen. If it
  says "Tap the video to start it", tap the video once.
- **Recording:** UltiUP doesn't record meetings yet. Record with OBS (or
  similar), upload the recording to YouTube, and add the link in
  **Videos** once YouTube has finished processing it.
- **Volume:** tap a person to make them louder or quieter (0–200%) or mute
  them, just for you. Settings (⚙) changes devices mid-call and the
  overall volume. UltiUP remembers these on this device.

### Events

Upcoming USA Ultimate tournaments. Filter the list, and tap a tournament for
its details and a link to USA Ultimate.

If USA Ultimate has posted a schedule, the tournament page also shows
**Schedule & brackets**. Pick a division (Men, Mixed, Women), then a stage
tab: pool standings and games, crossovers, and the championship and
placement brackets drawn as a tree (scroll sideways for earlier rounds).
Winners are highlighted green. Tap refresh for the latest scores during an
event.

### Rankings

USA Ultimate rankings by division. Tap a team for its USA Ultimate page.

## Privacy

- **Personal events** are visible only to you.
- **Paid status and USA Ultimate status** on the roster are visible only to
  owners, coaches and captains.
- **Direct messages** are visible only to the two people in them. Anyone
  signed in can find you by name to message you; your email is never shown.
- **Chat rooms**, their messages, photos and files are visible only to the
  people the room gives access to (and the team owner).
- **Notifications** and your notification settings are visible only to
  you.
- **The invite code** is visible only to owners, coaches and captains.
  Anyone with the code can join as a player, so share it only with your team.
  The owner can make a new code at any time; the old one stops working.

These rules are enforced by the server, not just hidden in the app.

## USA Ultimate data

USA Ultimate has no public API. UltiUP reads tournaments, rankings and team
pages (including event schedules and brackets) from play.usaultimate.org and
links back to it. Information may be out of date or incomplete; check USA
Ultimate for anything official.
