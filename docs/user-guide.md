# Stall 10 user guide

Stall 10 keeps your ultimate team in one place: schedule, roster, playbook,
stats, chat and pickup groups. This guide covers
version 0.0.12. What changed in each version is in
[release-notes.md](release-notes.md).

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** with your first and last name, a **username**, your email
   and a password (twice), or with Google or Apple. (Google and Apple
   aren't available on Windows.)
   - Your **username** is how people find and message you, as @name. It's
     3–20 lowercase letters, numbers, `_` or `.`, and only one person can
     have each; the form tells you as you type if it's taken. Signing up
     with Google or Apple asks you to pick one before you start.
   - **Passwords** need at least 8 characters with a letter and a number,
     and can't be a common password. The bar under the box shows how
     strong it is; the eye shows what you typed.
   - The app asks once whether to send you **notifications**, and again
     when you join a team or follow someone.
2. **Join or create a team.** On Home or the Teams tab:
   - **Join with code**: enter the 6-character invite code your team admin
     shows you. Codes change every 30 seconds, so enter it while they have
     it up. You join as a player.
   - **Invite link**: tap a link someone sent you (in Messages, WhatsApp,
     email or a Stall 10 message). It opens Stall 10 and shows the team;
     tap **Join**. Links expire (1 hour to 30 days, the sender picks).
   - **Create team**: you become its owner. Show the invite code or share
     an invite link from the team's **Members** page.
   Sign in later with your email **or** your @username.
3. **Profile** is the last tab. **Edit profile** changes what others
   see; the **gear** at the top right opens [Settings](#settings).

## Roles

Every team has an **Owner** and **Players**. The owner creates any other
roles (Coach, Captain, Stats, anything) on the team's **Roles** page, and
picks what each one can do:

| Permission         | Lets someone…                                          |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| **Manage team**    | Edit details, the roster, playbook, posts and videos   |
| **Manage members** | Invite, approve and remove members                     |
| **Manage chats**   | Create, edit and delete chat rooms and folders         |
| **Manage events**  | Run events, meetings and tournaments                   |
| **Moderate**       | Delete other people's chat messages                    |
| **Keep stats**     | Track games and edit stats (teams only)                |

Give someone a role from **Members**. A person can have several roles and
gets everything they allow. Players without a role can see the team,
RSVP, chat where rooms let them, and play back plays. Only the owner
creates roles, transfers the team or deletes it.

Teams from before custom roles had Coach and Captain. They become custom
roles named Coach and Captain (Organizer in groups), with every
permission, the first time the owner opens the team, and chat rooms that
were open to them stay open.

You can also **import roles and channels from a Discord server template**
link. In a school or club community with teams, see
[Schools and clubs](#schools-and-clubs) for how rooms are sorted into
teams.

Anyone can leave a team from the ⋮ menu on its home page (the owner must
transfer ownership first). If an owner deletes their account, the team
passes to another member, preferring one with Manage team.

## The tabs

### Home

Home has the week, your team, then your **feed**. On a wide screen,
your events and playbook sit beside the feed.

- **This week**: the next seven days. Dots under each day show events:
  **blue for team**, **purple for personal**. Each event has a TEAM or
  PERSONAL tag (or the team's name if you're on several teams). Tap a day or
  **Full schedule** to open the Schedule tab.
- **My team**: your team at a glance. If you're on several teams, switch
  with the chips.
- **Feed**: posts from you, your teams and groups, and everything you
  follow, newest first. Tap **Share an update or workout** to post. See
  [Following and posts](#following-and-posts).
- **Playbook** (wide screens): a preview of your team's plays. Tap one to
  open it.
- **Coming up** (wide screens): your next team and personal events.

### Search

The search box at the top of Home (phones) or of every page (tablets and
computers) searches **players** (by name or @username), **teams** and
**pickup groups**. Results open in **Explore**, where chips narrow them to
one kind. Explore also has shortcuts to starting a team or group.

On a computer, press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) anywhere for the
**command menu**: type to jump to a section or one of your teams, start a
post or message, record a workout, or search everything. Arrow keys
move, Enter opens, Esc closes. **Ctrl 1–5** switch between Home,
Explore, Team, Messages and Profile.

### Following and posts

- **Follow** a person, team, community group or group tournament from its
  page. Their posts then show up in your Home
  feed. Posts from your own teams and groups are always there.
- Tap the **bell** next to **Following** to be notified when they post. To
  turn every post notification off, use **Posts you follow** in
  notification settings.
- **Following** (Settings → People you follow, or **Following** above the
  feed) lists everything you follow, with each bell and **Unfollow** in
  each row's ⋮ menu.
- After you follow someone, the app offers to notify you when they post.
  Change it later from **Following ▾ → Post notifications** on their
  profile.
- **Write a post**: text, up to 4 photos and 5 links. Anyone signed in can
  see your posts on your profile. Optionally tag a tournament you follow, so its followers see the post too.
- **Share a workout**: pick the activity, then add how long it took, an
  optional distance and effort, and any exercises with sets, reps and
  weight. Runs with a distance show your pace.
- **Record a run or ride** (phones): tap **Record** on Home or your
  profile. Pick Run, Ride, Walk or Hike, then **Start**. The map draws your
  route as you go, with your time, distance and pace (speed for rides).
  Recording keeps going with the screen locked or while you use the rest
  of the app; the Record button shows the clock so you can get back. Pause,
  then **Finish** to share it. The post shows the route map, and opening it
  shows your time for each mile (or km). **Hide your start and finish** is
  on by default: it cuts the first and last 200 m from the map so it
  doesn't show where you live. Your stats don't change.
- **Profiles**: tap a post's author, someone in a team's Members list, or
  **View profile** in New message. You'll see their posts and follower
  counts, and buttons to Follow or Message them. Edit or delete your own
  posts from the ⋮ on each one. Deleting happens at once with **Undo**
  for a few seconds, the same for comments and chat messages.

### Notifications

Tap the 🔔 at the top of Home to see your notifications. A badge shows how
many are unread. Tap one to open it; **Mark all read** clears the badge.

You're notified about:

- **Team chat**: new messages in rooms you can read. Several messages in
  one room fold into one notification.
- **Direct messages**: new private messages.
- **Event changes**: team events added, moved, changed or cancelled (not
  RSVPs or reactions).
- **Event reminders**: a reminder about a day before each team or personal
  event, unless you've said you can't go.
- **Team activity**: new and updated plays in the playbook.
- **Posts you follow**: new posts from people, teams and tournaments whose
  bell you turned on.
- **Meetings**: meetings you're invited to, cancellations, and a heads-up
  when one you're attending starts.

You aren't notified about things you did yourself. While Stall 10 is
open, new ones also pop up as a small bubble at the top.

**Settings** (the ⋮ menu on the notifications page, or Settings →
Notifications):

- **This device**: tap **Turn on** to get push notifications on your phone,
  tablet or Mac, even when Stall 10 is closed. Windows has no push, but
  everything still shows under the 🔔.
- **Notify me about**: turn each kind on or off for all your teams. Your
  choices apply on every device you use.
- **Teams**: change any kind for one team, for example reminders only for
  your club team. **Use my defaults** undoes that.
- To mute one chat room, open the room's **⋮** menu and tap **Mute notifications**.

### Messages

Open the **Messages** tab for your private messages. A badge shows how
many conversations have something new.

- **New message**: search for anyone on Stall 10 by name or @username,
  then tap them to start a conversation. You don't need to share a team.
- Only the two of you can see a conversation. Attach photos or files up to
  10 MB, the same as team chat. You can delete your own messages.
- On a computer, conversations are listed on the left with the open one
  on the right. On a phone, a conversation opens full screen; the back
  arrow returns to Messages.
- **Seen** shows under your last message once the other person has read
  it. Turn **Read receipts** off in Settings → Privacy; then they don't
  see yours and you don't see theirs.
- Search shows names, @usernames and photos only, never email addresses.

### Teams

Your teams. Tap one to open its home page: the team at a glance, what's
**Up next**, and the team's **Posts**. Owners, coaches and captains tap
**New post** to share news (tryouts, fees, field changes). Each post is
**Public** or for members only; admins can edit or delete posts.

Members get a tab bar under the team name: **Home** (the above), then
one tab for each part of the team. Switching tabs keeps you on the team
page; back takes you out of the team. The tabs:

- **Team Events**: the team calendar. Open an event to RSVP **Going**,
  **Maybe** or **Can't go**. Once you're going, you can react and vote in
  the event's poll. Admins can make an event **Public** to show it on the
  team's public page; RSVPs and polls stay private.
- **Chat**: the team's chat rooms. Every team has a **General** room for
  everyone. Owners, coaches and captains add rooms (say, Captains or
  Carpool) and choose, per role and per person, who can read and who can
  post. Attach photos or files up to 10 MB; tap a photo to enlarge or save
  it, tap a file to save it. You can delete your own messages; the owner can
  delete any.
  On a computer, rooms are listed on the left and the open room is on the
  right. On a phone, tap a room to slide it open; the 💬 button at the top
  left opens your room list, and tapping the open room takes you back.
- **Roster**: players with number and position. Admins also see and mark
  who's paid, and can filter to **Unpaid only**.
- **Playbook**: the team's plays. Admins draw plays on the field and add
  animation steps (drag a player to record a cut, the disc to record a
  throw); everyone can play them back.
- **Stats**: tournaments and games. Stat keepers track points live (lines,
  O/D, pull timer, subs, timeouts); everyone can view stat sheets and
  season stats.
- **Members**: who's on the team and their roles. Admins tap **Show
  code** for the invite code: a new one every 30 seconds, each working for
  45, with a countdown. **Hide** stops it. **Invite** players: pick how
  long the link works (1 hour, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days; 7 days unless you
  change it), then copy or **Share** it to any app, or search for someone
  on Stall 10 to send them an in-app invite plus the link in a message.
  Anyone with a live link can join as a player. A group's link opens its
  page, where people join or ask to, and doesn't expire.

**Find a team**: tap 🔍 at the top of the Teams tab (or search from Home)
and type part of a team's name or city. Tap a team to see its public page: name, division and
city, plus any public events and posts. Members, rosters and chat stay
private. Tap **Join** and enter the invite code an admin shows you.

### Community groups

A community group is an open pickup community: no roster or stats, just
**Events**, **Chat**, **Tournaments** and **Members**. In a group, roles
are **Owner**, **Organizer** (like a captain: runs events and
tournaments) and **Member**.

- **Start one**: Teams tab → **New** → **Community group**. Add a
  description and a location (search an address, then tap the map to
  fine-tune). Turn on **Approve new members** to review who joins.
- **Find one**: tap 🔍 in the Teams tab and choose **Groups**. Open a group
  to see its description and map, then tap **Join** (or **Ask to join**,
  if it needs approval). Organizers approve requests on the Members page.
- **Pickup** events work like any team event. For **Tournament** events
  the RSVPs are **Attending**, **Planning to attend** and **Not able to
  make it :(**.

**Running a hat tournament** (owners and organizers; teams made from
RSVPs, listed under **Hat tournaments** in the Tournaments section):

1. Post a Tournament event so people can RSVP. On the event, tap **Set up
   tournament** (or **New tournament** on the Tournaments page). Every
   tournament shows on the group schedule: one set up without an event
   gets a Tournament event when you save, and a linked event's title and
   times follow the tournament's. The event then has a **View** button
   for the tournament.
2. Set the **dates**: the first and last day of play. Each day gets its
   own **start time**; tap a day to change it. Add an ideal end if you
   like.
3. Pick the format (round robin, pool play + single elimination, single
   elimination, double elimination) and the number of **teams** (they
   start as Team 1, Team 2… and you can rename them), plus players on the
   field (3v3 to 7v7), game length, breaks, score cap, timeouts and fields.
4. Check the **schedule preview**. Games are split evenly over the days,
   in bracket order. It warns if games run past the ideal end. Tap
   **Publish schedule**. Teams can still be empty at this point. The first
   time you publish, Stall 10 offers to **post it to the group feed** with
   the dates, format and how to sign up filled in. The post is public, so
   people who haven't joined yet see it too; edit it before posting.
5. Add players, now or any time before play: **Import RSVPs** brings in
   everyone attending or planning to attend, **Add member** adds anyone
   else, and **Assign teams** deals them all evenly into the teams at
   random, each with a random captain. Tap a player to move them or make
   them captain. Tap **Save**; the schedule stays as it is. Changing the
   number of teams means publishing the schedule again.

**Playing**: the tournament shows up for everyone on a team. Open it for
the schedule, standings, bracket and teams; your games are highlighted.
Each team's **captain** taps a game to **Report score**. The other captain
then **confirms** it, or taps **That's not right** to report their own
score for the first captain to confirm. Organizers can set or correct any
score. Once a score is final, winners (and losers, in double elimination)
move on in the bracket. With pool play, organizers tap **Pools are done:
seed the bracket** when every pool game is final.

### Hosting a tournament

Any team, community group or school/club can host a tournament for other
teams, from its **Tournaments** section. People with **Manage events**
run it.

1. Tap **Host a tournament**: name, dates, location, about, **Public**
   (any team can apply) or **Invite only** (only teams you invite play),
   and an optional **bid fee**: amount, how to pay and a due date. Stall
   10 doesn't take payments; teams pay you and you mark them paid.
2. It starts as a **draft** only your managers see. On its page, **Add**
   divisions: level and division (e.g. Club Mixed), team cap, format and
   game rules (game to, timeouts, length, fields, first game).
   **Suggest** picks the usual format for the cap: a round robin for 5
   or fewer, otherwise pools of 4–5 and a placement bracket.
3. **Publish and open registration**. The page, schedule, results and
   news are public either way.
4. **Teams** tab: accept, waitlist or decline applications; **Invite**
   searches for teams. Accepted teams show the bid fee **Paid** switch;
   **Unpaid only** filters, and unpaid teams past the due date show
   **Overdue**.
5. **Close registration**, then drag teams into seed order (1 is the
   strongest). Pools are snake-seeded and brackets follow pool finish.
6. **Schedule** tab: check the preview and **Publish schedule** for each
   division. Republishing regenerates every game from the seeds.
7. Captains report and confirm scores; you can set or correct any score.
   When every game is played, the **Results** tab shows the champion and
   final places.
8. **News**: **Post update** puts a public post on your page, tagged
   with the tournament, so its followers see it.

**Playing in one**: find tournaments in **Explore → Find a tournament**.
A team's owner or captain taps **Apply with a team**, picks the division
and up to 4 captains (they report scores), or **Accept invite** if
invited. The Overview tab shows your status and whether your bid fee is
marked paid.

### Schools and clubs

A **School or club** community holds several full teams, like a college
with a men's team and a women's team. Start one from Teams → **New** →
**School or club**. Its **Teams** tab lists its teams.

- **Add teams**: tap **New team** to make one inside the community, or
  have an existing team's owner open its ⋮ menu → **School or club** and
  ask to join; accept the request on the community's Teams tab.
- **Who's on each team**: for each team pick the community roles that get
  onto it (say Woof Captain, Woof Coach, Woof Player for Woof). People
  with one of those roles join the team automatically, and leave it when
  the role is taken away. Teams show **Joining…** for a moment while
  that happens.
- **Events**: a community event can be for everyone or only some roles
  (**Who sees it** in the event editor).
- **Discord import**: importing a server into the community sorts its
  rooms. A room only one team's roles can read goes to that team's chat;
  rooms everyone or several teams read stay in the community chat. Change
  any room with its dropdown before importing. Each team gets copies of
  its community roles (marked "given out by" the community on the Roles
  page), and who holds them follows the community. Rooms only go to teams
  you own. Rooms already in the community chat from an earlier import
  are moved, after asking.
- A team can leave its community from ⋮ → **School or club**; people the
  community added leave with it.

### Schedule

Your whole week in one place: personal events plus every team you're on.

- Use the arrows to move between weeks, and **Today** to come back.
- Tap a day in the strip to jump to it.
- Filter with **All**, **Team** or **Personal**.
- **New event** adds an event on the selected day. If you're an owner,
  coach or captain, choose whether it goes on **My schedule** or a team's
  schedule.
- **Swipe an event right to delete it.** Tap **Undo** within 4 seconds to
  keep it. You can always swipe your personal events; team events only if
  you're an owner, coach or captain. Others can't be swiped.

Event types: Practice, Game, Tournament, Scrimmage, Workout, Social, Other
(in groups: Pickup, Tournament, Social, Other).
Personal events are private: only you can see them.

### Videos

Videos shared with your team, like meeting recordings or game film. Open
your team and tap **Videos**.

- **Watch:** tap a video to play it in the app. For YouTube videos,
  **Open on YouTube** plays it on YouTube instead.
- **Add one** (owners, coaches and captains):
  1. Upload the video to YouTube. Set it to **Unlisted** if it's just for
     the team, and leave **Allow embedding** on.
  2. In **Videos**, tap **Add video** and paste the link. Stall 10 checks it
     with YouTube, shows the thumbnail and fills in the title.
  3. Change the title if you like, add a description, and tap
     **Add video**.
- **Or upload a video file:** tap **Add video**, choose **Upload file**,
  then **Choose video file**. MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, MPEG and 3GP
  all work. Files can be up to 500 MB, and each team has 5 GB in
  all; the bar above the videos shows how much is used. Keep the page
  open until the upload finishes.
- If Stall 10 says YouTube won't play the video here, it's set to Private,
  it was deleted, or embedding is turned off. Fix that on YouTube and
  paste the link again.
- **Folders:** tap a chip above the videos (**All**, a folder, or
  **Unsorted**) to see just those. Owners, coaches and captains tap
  **New folder** to make one (say Film review, Game film or Highlights),
  and **⋮ → Move to folder** on a video to file it. With a folder open,
  **Add video** puts new videos in it, and the **⋮** next to the chips
  renames or deletes it. Deleting a folder doesn't delete its videos;
  they move to Unsorted.
- Use **⋮ → Edit** or **Remove** on a video to change or unlist it.
  Removing a YouTube video here doesn't delete it from YouTube. Removing
  an upload deletes the file and frees its space.
- Unlisted videos aren't searchable on YouTube, but anyone who has the
  link can watch, so only share film you're happy to have passed along.

### Meetings

Video meetings for your team. They show on the schedule with a 🎥 icon,
but only to the people invited.

- **Schedule one** (owners, coaches and captains): on the team's Events
  tab tap **New** → **Meeting**. Give it a title, time and agenda, then
  pick who's invited: whole roles (Admins are always in), particular
  people, or both. Turn on **Require admin approval to join** to let people
  in one at a time.
- **RSVP** on the meeting page: **Attending** or **Not attending**. Admins
  see everyone's answers.
- **Go to meeting room** opens the lobby. Pick your microphone, speaker
  and camera, check your camera preview and set the meeting volume. Stall 10
  asks for camera and microphone access the first time.
- **Starting:** an admin taps **Start meeting** (or **Start meeting early**
  before the start time). Everyone attending who isn't already there gets a
  notification, or a banner with **Join** if Stall 10 is open.
- **Getting in:** without approval, tap **Join meeting** once it's started.
  With approval, tap **Ask to join** and wait; admins see the waiting list
  in the lobby and in the call, and tap **Admit** or **Deny**.
- **In the call** you'll see everyone's camera, or their photo when it's
  off; a green border shows who's talking. The buttons along the bottom:
  mic, camera, mute all sound, share screen (Windows, Mac and Android),
  chat, settings and **Leave**. Admins can also **End for everyone**.
- **Chat** lets you type instead of unmuting. Only people in the call can
  read it.
- **Watch a video together** (owners, coaches and captains): tap the
  videos button along the bottom and pick one of the team's videos (filter
  by folder). It fills the screen for everyone, with people in a strip
  beside it. Use **Play**, **Pause** and the slider; everyone's video
  follows yours. **Stop** goes back to the call. Another admin can tap
  **Take control** to drive. Everyone hears the video through their own
  device at full quality, so there's no need to share your screen. If it
  says "Tap the video to start it", tap the video once.
- **Recording:** Stall 10 doesn't record meetings yet. Record with OBS (or
  similar), upload the recording to YouTube, and add the link in
  **Videos** once YouTube has finished processing it.
- **Volume:** tap a person to make them louder or quieter (0–200%) or mute
  them, just for you. Settings (⚙) changes devices mid-call and the
  overall volume. Stall 10 remembers these on this device.

## Settings

Tap the **gear** at the top right of your profile. Settings slides in
from the right (on a computer, as a panel; click outside to close it).

- **General**: the theme: **System** (follows your device), **Light** or
  **Dark**.
- **Notifications**: push on this device, and which notifications you get
  (see [Notifications](#notifications)).
- **Privacy**: **Private profile**, **Read receipts**, and what others
  see on your profile (stats, teams, games). **Follow requests** lists
  people asking to follow a private profile: **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **People you follow**.
- **Account**: change your **display name**; see your **@username** and
  **email**; **Change password** (or "Forgot it?" for a reset link); **Delete account** (it asks you to type DELETE and confirm
  your password, or sign in with Google or Apple again).
- **Sign out**, and the app version at the bottom.

## Privacy

- **Personal events** are visible only to you.
- **Paid status** on the roster is visible only to
  the owner and people whose role has **Manage team**.
- **Direct messages** are visible only to the two people in them. Anyone
  signed in can find you by name or @username to message you; your email
  is never shown. With read receipts off, nobody sees when you've read
  their messages.
- **Your username** is public to anyone signed in, and only you can set
  it.
- **Chat rooms**, their messages, photos and files are visible only to the
  people the room gives access to (and the team owner).
- **Notifications** and your notification settings are visible only to
  you.
- **Private profile**: others see only your name, @username and photo
  until you accept their follow request.
- **Your posts** (and their photos) are public to anyone signed in to
  Stall 10. **Who you follow** is private: others see only how many people
  follow you and how many you follow.
- **Invite codes and links** come only from the owner and people whose
  role has **Manage members**. Anyone with a live code or link can join as a player, so share
  them only with your team. Codes stop working 45 seconds after they
  appear; links when their time is up. Codes from before codes rotated no
  longer work.

These rules are enforced by the server, not just hidden in the app.
