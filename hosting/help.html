Getting started

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse the community feed, search for players and teams, or see what's coming up. To post, like, follow, or join a team, create a free account.

Where do I get the app?

The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon; see Download. The app has everything: team chats, schedules, playbooks, stats and meetings.

Teams and groups

How do I join my team?

Ask a team admin for an invite link or code, or find the team in Explore and ask to join.

What's the difference between a team and a group?

A team has a roster, schedule, playbook and stats. A group is for pickup, leagues and communities: anyone can find it, and it can be open, or approve new members first.

Privacy and safety

Who can see my profile and posts?

By default, anyone, including people browsing the website. To limit that, make your profile private in the app (Settings → Privacy); then only followers you approve see your posts, teams and stats. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile and everything you've posted.

How do I report something?

Email [contact email] with a link to the post or profile and what's wrong. [Update this once the app has a report button.]
