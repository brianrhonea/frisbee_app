Community guidelines

Stall 10 is for ultimate players of every age, including teenagers. Play it like Spirit of the Game: be fair, be kind, and respect the people you share the field with. [Draft: have this reviewed before launch.]

What isn't allowed

  • Bullying and harassment: insults, threats, piling on, or messaging someone who has made clear they don't want it.
  • Hate: attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  • Sexual content: nudity, sexual messages or images, and anything sexual involving anyone under 18, ever.
  • Putting young players at risk: adults asking minors for photos, personal details or to meet in private, or moving them to other apps.
  • Violence and self-harm: threats, glorifying violence, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
  • Spam and scams: fake accounts, selling followers, phishing, and repeated promotion.
  • Pretending to be someone else, a team, or a tournament organizer.
  • Other people's private information: addresses, phone numbers or photos shared without their permission.
  • Anything illegal, or content you don't have the rights to share.

Words and photos we filter

Stall 10 checks what you write before it's posted. Slurs, hate speech and telling someone to hurt themselves aren't posted at all: you'll be asked to change the words. Swearing is allowed but shows masked (f***), so younger players can use the app too.

Photos are checked as they're uploaded. Nudity and sexual or graphic violent images are refused; if one slips through, report it.

Report and block

Every post, comment, message and profile has Report (in the ⋯ menu, or the message's options). Pick a reason and, if you like, block the person at the same time. We never tell anyone who reported them. Reports on a team's chat and posts also go to that team's admins, who can remove them.

Block someone from their profile or your conversation with them: you stop seeing each other's posts, comments and messages, and neither of you can follow or message the other. Teams you share still work. Unblock anyone in Settings → Blocked accounts.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

What we do

Stall 10's team reviews every report. Depending on what happened we remove the content, warn the person, or suspend their account; serious or repeated breaks lose the account for good.
