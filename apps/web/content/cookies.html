Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This page explains how the Stall 10 website stores information in your browser. The short version: we only store what's needed to make the site work. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and nothing tracks you across other sites.

What we store

WhatWhyHow long
Sign-in session (Firebase Authentication, in your browser's IndexedDB)Keeps you signed in between visits. Until you log out
Theme preference (local storage)Remembers light or dark mode.Until you clear it
App files (browser cache)Loads the site faster on your next visit.Until updated or cleared

These are strictly necessary for the site to work, so most cookie laws don't require your consent for them.

Third parties

Signing in with Google opens a Google window, which uses Google's own cookies. A YouTube video in a post may set YouTube cookies when it plays. Google's policies cover those.

Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser settings at any time; you'll be signed out. Blocking all site storage stops sign-in from working.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle your information.
