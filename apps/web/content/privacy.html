Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Stall 10 ("we", "us") is an app and website for ultimate frisbee teams, groups and players. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It covers the Stall 10 apps for iPhone, Android, macOS and Windows and the website at [your domain].

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (your account, profile, posts, team activity) and what's needed to run the service (like a device token for notifications).
  • Your profile and posts are public unless you make your profile private. Team chats, schedules, playbooks and messages are only for the people in them.
  • We don't sell your information or show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account, and what you've created, from the app at any time.

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, username and password, or the name and email Google or Apple share when you sign in with them.
  • Profile: anything you add, such as a photo, bio, pronouns, hometown, school, throwing hand, the year you started playing, your teams, and stats your teams record.
  • Content: posts, photos, comments, likes, reposts, follows, team posts, chat and direct messages, events and RSVPs, plays, videos and links you share.
  • Teams and groups: the teams you create or join, your role, and your roster details (like jersey number and position).
  • Location, only when you choose: if you record a run or ride, the app uses your phone's GPS while recording, and a workout you post can include its route (people browsing the website without an account see the workout but not the route). A group can set a meeting place on a map. We don't track your location otherwise.
  • Meetings and voice: audio and video in team meetings and voice channels are streamed live between participants. Stall 10 doesn't record them.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and notification data: a push notification token and an app installation ID, so we can send the notifications you've turned on.
  • Security data: the apps use Firebase App Check to confirm requests come from a genuine app. To stop abuse, we count actions (like posts or searches) per account and, for website visitors who aren't signed in, per a one-way hash of their IP address (we don't store the address itself).
  • Service logs: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and time of request) for security and troubleshooting.
  • On your device: the app and website remember that you're signed in and your theme choice. See our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To provide Stall 10: show your profile and posts, run your teams' schedules, chats, playbooks, stats and meetings, and let people find you and your teams.
  • To send notifications you've asked for.
  • To keep Stall 10 safe: prevent spam, scraping, flooding and abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • To fix problems and improve the service.

We don't sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Who can see what

  • Public by default: your name, username, photo, profile details, teams, stats and posts can be seen by anyone, including people browsing the website without an account. Your profile settings let you hide your teams, stats and games.
  • Private profiles: if you make your profile private, people who aren't approved followers (and website visitors) see only your name, username and photo.
  • Teams and groups: a team's directory listing, public posts and public events can be seen by anyone, unless it's a private group. Chats, member-only posts and events, playbooks, videos, stats and meetings are only for members.
  • Direct messages are only for the people in the conversation.

Services we use

We use these providers to run Stall 10. They handle data on our behalf, or as needed to provide their feature:

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud): sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, hosting, push notifications and App Check.
  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.
  • LiveKit: live audio and video for meetings and voice channels.
  • YouTube: videos linked from YouTube play in YouTube's player, under Google's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: maps, and looking up addresses you type for group locations.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app (Settings → Account), we delete your account, profile and the content you created, including your posts, comments and photos. Teams you own pass to another admin, or are deleted if nobody else is on them. Messages you sent are deleted too; other people keep their conversations and their own messages, with you shown as "Deleted user". Backups and logs are cleared on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete your profile details and posts at any time.
  • Make your profile private, and hide your teams, stats or games.
  • Turn notifications on or off in the app or your device settings.
  • Delete your account from the app, or as described on Delete your account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, or to object to some uses. To ask, email us at [contact email].

Security
