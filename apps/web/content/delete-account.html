Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 6, 2026

In the app

  1. Open Stall 10 and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account (or Settings → Legal & your data → Delete account & data).
  3. Tap Delete account, confirm who you are, and confirm again.

Deletion starts straight away and can't be undone.

Can't use the app?

Email [contact email] from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and profile, including your photos, stats and settings.
  • Your posts and comments, and the photos and videos you uploaded.
  • The messages you sent, in direct messages, groups and team chats.
  • Your notifications, and your place on teams and in groups.

What stays

  • Other people keep their conversations with you, and their own messages in them; you're shown there as "Deleted user".
  • Teams you own pass to another admin, or are deleted if nobody else is on them.
  • Backups and logs are cleared on their normal schedule, and we keep what the law requires us to.

See the Privacy Policy for more.
